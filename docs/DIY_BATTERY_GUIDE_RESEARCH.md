#### 4. Understanding Battery Basics (400 words)
- **H2**: "18650 vs 21700 vs LiFePO4: Choosing Your Cells"
- **H3**: "18650 Cells"
  - Capacity: 2,500-3,500 mAh
  - Voltage: 3.6-3.7V nominal
  - Pros: Widely available, cheaper
  - Cons: Lower capacity than 21700
- **H3**: "21700 Cells"
  - Capacity: 4,000-5,000 mAh
  - Better energy density
  - Used in Tesla vehicles
- **H3**: "Series vs Parallel Configuration"
  - Voltage calculation (series)
  - Capacity calculation (parallel)
  - Example: 13S4P = 48V, 10kWh pack
