# Portugal Solar Irradiance Data Reference

## Overview
Portugal receives some of the highest levels of solar irradiation in Europe, with 2,200-3,000 hours of sunshine annually.

## Data Sources
- **PVGIS (Photovoltaic Geographical Information System)**: EU Joint Research Centre's official tool
- **Global Solar Atlas**: globalsolaratlas.info/download/portugal
- **Solargis**: Free solar radiation maps and GIS data for Portugal

## Regional Solar Data

### Lisbon (Central Portugal)
- **Annual Yield**: 1,511 kWh/year per kWp
- **Peak Sun Hours**: ~4.14 hours/day (1511 ÷ 365)
- **Daily Irradiance**: 4.1-4.5 kWh/m²/day

### Porto (North Portugal)
- **Peak Sun Hours**: ~3.8-4.2 hours/day
- **Daily Irradiance**: 3.8-4.2 kWh/m²/day
- **Characteristics**: More rainfall, slightly less solar than south

### Algarve (Faro, Lagos, Southern Portugal)
- **Peak Sun Hours**: ~5.0-5.5 hours/day
- **Daily Irradiance**: 5.0-5.5 kWh/m²/day
- **Characteristics**: Highest solar potential in Portugal, Mediterranean climate

### Azores Islands
- **Peak Sun Hours**: ~3.5-4.0 hours/day
- **Daily Irradiance**: 3.5-4.0 kWh/m²/day
- **Characteristics**: Atlantic climate, more cloud cover

### Madeira Island
- **Peak Sun Hours**: ~4.8-5.2 hours/day
- **Daily Irradiance**: 4.8-5.2 kWh/m²/day
- **Characteristics**: Subtropical climate, excellent solar potential

## Monthly Irradiance Patterns (Lisbon Example)
Based on PVGIS data, monthly variations follow Mediterranean patterns:
- **Winter (Dec-Feb)**: 2.5-3.5 kWh/m²/day
- **Spring (Mar-May)**: 4.5-6.0 kWh/m²/day
- **Summer (Jun-Aug)**: 6.5-7.5 kWh/m²/day
- **Autumn (Sep-Nov)**: 4.0-5.5 kWh/m²/day

## Climate Factors for Calculations
- **Coastal Areas**: 0.90-0.92 (humidity, salt air)
- **Inland Areas**: 0.92-0.95 (clearer air, less moisture)
- **Urban Areas**: 0.88-0.90 (pollution, dust)

## Usage Notes
1. Always use PVGIS for precise location-specific data: https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/photovoltaic-geographical-information-system-pvgis_en
2. Account for system losses: inverter (3-5%), wiring (2-3%), temperature (10-15%)
3. Portugal has excellent year-round solar potential compared to Northern Europe
4. Algarve region is optimal for maximum solar production
