## 🏗️ Site Restructure Strategy

### Free Tier (Lead Generation)

**Objective:** Drive traffic, build trust, capture emails, showcase value

**What's Free:**
1. **Basic Calculators** (100% free, no email required for basic results)
   - Panel Sizing Calculator (Portugal regions, basic output: # panels, system size kW)
   - Battery Sizing Calculator (basic capacity recommendation)
   - Load Analysis Calculator (appliance list, daily kWh total)
   - Inverter Size Calculator (basic wattage recommendation)

2. **Educational Content** (First 2-3 sections of each guide)
   - DIY Battery Building: Sections 1-3 (Why DIY, Safety, Cell Selection) FREE
   - Solar Panel Installation: Introduction, Safety, Tools Required
   - Portugal Solar Guide: All regional data, permits overview

3. **Lead Magnets** (Capture email, deliver PDF)
   - "Portugal Solar Installation Checklist 2025" (47-point PDF)
   - "Complete Solar System Shopping List Template" (Excel)
   - "Monthly Solar Production Calculator" (Portugal regions)

**Conversion Triggers:**
- After 3rd calculator use: Modal "Save your designs + unlock advanced features with free account"
- When clicking "Download PDF Report": Email capture form
- When viewing locked DIY section: Preview first 100 words + upgrade prompt

### Pro Tier (€29/month or €290/year - 17% discount)

**Objective:** Provide hands-on DIY value, product recommendations, community access

**What's Included:**
1. **Complete DIY Course Library**
   - Battery Building: All 12 sections with video tutorials
   - Solar Panel Installation: 8-part video course
   - Inverter Setup & Configuration: Step-by-step guide
   - Electrical Wiring & Safety: Complete wiring diagrams
   - Off-Grid Appliances: Efficiency guides, product reviews

2. **Advanced Calculators**
   - System Designer: Step 6 wizard with product comparisons
   - ROI Calculator: 25-year projection with maintenance costs, incentives
   - Shade Analysis Tool: Basic shading calculator
   - Wire Sizing Calculator: Voltage drop, ampacity tables

3. **Product Recommendations**
   - Curated supplier list (Portugal + EU shipping)
   - Affiliate discounts (5-15% off partnered suppliers)
   - Product comparison matrices (panels, batteries, inverters)
   - Monthly "Best Value" product rankings

4. **Community Access**
   - Private forum (ask questions, share projects)
   - Monthly live Q&A webinar
   - Member project showcase

5. **Downloadable Resources**
   - BOM templates (Excel, auto-calculated)
   - Installation checklists (printable PDFs)
   - Wiring diagram templates (Visio/Draw.io)

**Value Proposition:** "Everything you need to build your own solar system and save €5,000+ on installation costs"

### Expert Tier (€79/month or €790/year - 17% discount)

**Objective:** Custom design service, premium support, professional-grade tools

**What's Included (Pro + Expert-Exclusive):**
1. **Custom System Design Service**
   - One custom system design per month (BOM, wiring diagram, layout)
   - 30-minute video consultation with solar engineer
   - Review of your site photos/roof measurements
   - Optimized component selection for your specific needs

2. **Premium Tools**
   - Advanced Shade Analysis (satellite imagery integration)
   - 3D Roof Layout Designer (drag-drop panel placement)
   - Financial Modeling Tool (bank loan comparisons, tax incentives)
   - Energy Monitoring Dashboard (integrate with your system)

3. **Priority Support**
   - Email support with 24-hour response time
   - WhatsApp support group access
   - Troubleshooting assistance during installation

4. **Exclusive Content**
   - Advanced topics: Grid-tie vs off-grid, hybrid systems
   - Commercial system design course
   - Supplier negotiation tactics
   - Installation business startup guide

5. **Professional Network**
   - Verified installer directory (Portugal)
   - Bulk purchasing group (discounts on large orders)
   - Annual in-person meetup (Lisbon)

**Value Proposition:** "Professional-grade tools + expert guidance to design your perfect solar system with confidence"

### One-Time Purchases (No subscription required)

1. **Complete DIY Battery Course** - €149 (lifetime access)
   - All 12 sections, video tutorials, templates
   - For users who only need battery knowledge

2. **Custom System Design** - €299 (one-time)
   - Full professional design service
   - For users who don't want monthly subscription

3. **Installation Consultation** - €99/hour
   - Video call with NABCEP-certified engineer

## 📈 Success Metrics & KPIs

### Acquisition Metrics
- **Monthly Visitors:** Target 10,000 (Portugal solar searches, DIY content SEO)
- **Calculator Completion Rate:** Target 45% (industry standard 30-60%)
- **Email Capture Rate:** Target 25% of completed calculators (2,500 emails/month)
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Target €3 (mostly organic, some ads)

### Activation Metrics
- **Account Creation Rate:** Target 15% of email captures (375 accounts/month)
- **Core Activation Event:** Complete system design + save = activated user
- **Activation Rate:** Target 60% (225 activated users/month)

### Conversion Metrics
- **Free-to-Paid Conversion (Freemium):** Target 3% (113 conversions/month from 375 accounts)
- **Trial-to-Paid Conversion:** Target 15% (if offering 7-day trial)
- **Average Time to Conversion:** Target 14-21 days (nurture sequence)

### Retention & Revenue Metrics
- **Monthly Churn Rate:** Target <5% (excellent for SaaS)
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** Target €5,650 (113 users × €50 blended ARPU)
- **Annual Run Rate (ARR):** €67,800 first year (scaling to €300K+ at 10K signups)
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** €600 (€50 × 12 months avg subscription)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** 200:1 (€600 LTV / €3 CAC) - exceptional for organic

### Engagement Metrics
- **Daily Active Users (DAU):** Track calculator usage frequency
- **Monthly Active Users (MAU):** Track return visits
- **Feature Adoption:** % of Pro users accessing each feature (courses, forum, tools)
- **Content Completion:** % of users finishing DIY courses
